# Welcome to OVSD

**Open Virtual Stream Deck** turns any phone, tablet or browser into a fully customizable button panel to control your PC: keyboard shortcuts, music, volume, OBS, Discord, home automation, programs… and to show live information such as CPU usage, the song that is playing or whether you are live.

## How it works

```
 Phone / tablet (browser)  ──WiFi──►  OVSD on your PC (tray icon)
   shows the buttons                    runs the actions and works out
   and sends your taps                  what each button displays
```

- **The PC is in charge.** OVSD runs on Windows, keeps your profiles and runs the actions. The phone only shows the panel and sends your taps, so there is nothing to install on it.
- **Everything updates instantly.** Change something in the editor and the phone shows it right away. When a variable changes (the song, the OBS scene, the temperature…), the buttons that use it update by themselves.
- **Several devices at once**, each with its own profile.

## The building blocks

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | A complete panel: grid size (rows × columns), theme and pages. |
| **Page / folder** | Each screen of buttons in a profile. Folders get an automatic "back" key. |
| **Key** | What you place on the grid: a **button**, a **slider** or a **widget**. It can span several cells. |
| **Actions** | What a key does when you tap it, hold it, double-tap it… They can be sequences with delays and conditions (macros). |
| **Variables** | Live values (time, CPU, song, OBS, your own) you can show on keys or use in conditions. |
